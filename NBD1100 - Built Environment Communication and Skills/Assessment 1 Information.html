This is an individual assessment. The length of the essay should be controlled within 500 words, excluding references. The submitted version should be formatted following the in-class examples, including at least one image as a part of the essay. In-depth research and accurate referencing are required. The topic, analyses, and argument are expected to follow the instructions given in the class and be composed creatively with original thoughts.

Please use APA 7th referencing style. https://libraryguides.vu.edu.au/apa-referencing

Assessment Instructions

  1. Based on an initial search of available literature and resources, find a topic that you feel interested in and meaningful.
  2. Attend the group discussion with your classmates, sharing your ideas and knowledge.
  3. Investigate into one architectural case and focus on one question or topic.
  4. Draft the essay. You may refer to the rubric for more detailed information.
  5. Check the similarity rate with the pre-submission Turnitin Dropbox.
  6. Submit the completed essay to the online Dropbox before the due date.
