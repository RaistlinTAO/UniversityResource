This teamwork portfolio is a comprehensive project that contains a complete set of works including written reflections, posters, sketches and model making (please prioritise the physical model). In the posters, students are expected to integrate a concise introduction of the project, analyses and understanding into it. These contents need to be supported by diagrams, sketches and images. The 3D physical model is also made to serve to express your understanding of the specific project. Multiple features of the model, such as material selection, accuracy and craft will be evaluated. Students’ learning curve and progress will also be taken into consideration. TBC 40% Session 11

What are the Assessment Details?

This assessment is associated with other assessments in this unit. The essay would serve as a pilot study into a case. Assessment 2 mainly acts as a stage review of the project of Assessment 4. Assessment 3 (Presentation) is a showcase of what you have done in the project. The submission of this assessment includes models and posters where your sketches and text writing should be laid out nicely and professionally. Each team is required to have one common theme, and each student within the team is required to work on their individual case study that fits the common group theme. For instance, themes such as sustainable architecture around the globe, minimalist architecture, advanced construction techniques, and libraries in Australia were previous choices made by students. Be creative with your group theme. Students are required to use a common visual language within the group. You can look at the previous assignment examples of how this can be achieved.  

 

Assessment Instructions

  1. Based on what you found and the materials that you have collected around your chosen case study complete multiple drawings and sketches. For the skills, tips and instructed training in drawing and sketching, please attend the corresponding sessions and refer to the materials provided in the learning space.
  2. At the same time, work on the model-making to represent the case project with details. Make sure to take stage photos of the model-making process. For the skills, tips and instructed training for model making, please attend the corresponding sessions and refer to the materials provided in the learning space.
  3. Bring what you have done for the review in Sessions 6 and 8. Make use of the feedback gained from these reviews and try to refine your work before the final submission.
  4. Develop more analysis and drawings after Sessions 6 and 8. Also, complete the physical model.
  5. Document and submit all the required works in Session 11.

Please submit one packaged InDesign file as a group, including one exported PDF version. 

   

Assessment Criteria

Please refer to the rubric which is available in the assessment module.

Assessment 4 - Project (Group Portfolio) Checklist: An accumulated portfolio built upon Assignment 2 Stage 1 and Stage 2
  1. Digital analysis diagrams – in total three per person (one site analysis, one floor plan analysis and one vertical analysis)
  2. Hand-drawn perspective drawings – in total three per person  (one exterior, one interior, one view of your own choice, students may also choose to do construction detail sketches)
  3. A completed physical model - one per person
  4. A written final reflection (500 words per person) on what you have learned through the entire process of the case study
  5. You are encouraged to also curate the portfolio with real-life photos of the chosen case study. 
*Group portfolio submission format: the group should put all individual portfolios into one file and submit it as a group portfolio
