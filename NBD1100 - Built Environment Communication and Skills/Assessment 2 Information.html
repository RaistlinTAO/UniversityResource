This assessment is early review of the final project. It is separated into two stages (15% for each) to review the progress of the final project. Please be reminded that this is an individual assessment.

Students are expected to:

Demonstrate their ability to use the software according to the checklist learning. InDesign and Photoshop will be used in this unit.

Demonstrate their ability to analyse and communicate the chosen case study. The contents that need to be produced include sketches, diagrams and a physical model		 TBC 30% Stage 1: Session 6 - 3xInDesign Checklists

Stage 2: Session 8 - 3xPhotoshop Checklists

What are the Assessment Details?

InDesign Checklist x 3 (Stage 1)

Demonstrate the effective learning of the three checklists (text, image, grid line and A-parent page etc) and set up your InDesign portfolio to include the following contents:

  1. Two Hand-drawn perspective drawings per person - students may also choose to do one construction detail sketch
  2. Model-making plan and notes - one per person
  3. Indication of the group theme - a short reflection of 250 words and a graphic style plan or a group template

Please submit a packaged InDesign file, including a PDF exported version.

Photoshop Checklist x 3 (Stage 2)

Demonstrate the effective learning of the three checklists (line, shape, layer, legend and colour code etc) and produce the following contents:

  1. One floor plan analysis drawing per person
  2. One site analysis drawing per person

Please submit the original Photoshop file and an exported image file (JPEG/PNG).

 

Assessment Instructions

  1. Based on the investigation that you did for your essay, you can find more visual resources about your focused case study building. You can also choose a new case study aligning with your group theme.
  2. In InDesign, use an effective layout to document and curate your works.
  3. In Photoshop, use graphic language to further your analyses.
  4. Use a physical model to provide more information about your focused project.
  5. Submit your stage works to demonstrate your progress.

More information and guidance will be offered in the class.
