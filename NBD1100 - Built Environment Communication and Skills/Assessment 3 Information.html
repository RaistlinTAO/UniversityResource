What are the Assessment Details?

The presentation will be evaluated in the following aspects: structure, content, time management, support material, visual aids and body language. Each team is required to have one common theme, and each student within the team is required to work on their individual case study that fits the common group theme. For instance, themes such as sustainable architecture around the globe, minimalist architecture, advanced construction techniques, and libraries in Australia were previous choices made by students. The visual style should also have a group theme. You can choose to use a group template. Be creative with your group theme. 

 

Assessment Instructions

This is a presentation of your team project portfolio, which provides an opportunity to:

  1. Showcase your work.
  2. Demonstrate the knowledge you have acquired in this unit and how you applied them to your team project. For example, you can talk about what a challenge you met, how you addressed it and what your solution is.
  3. Demonstrate your collaboration as well as the individual role played in the team. Clarify your contribution.
  4. For specific criteria to guide your preparation, you may refer to the rubric of Assessment 3.
  5. Collect all posters from group members and submit the group A1 posters to the dropbox, you can use the Assessment 4 portfolio as presentation slides. 

Note: Prepare your presentation slides (portfolio) and poster in advance and bring to the lecture in Session 11.
