After Class Reading

After Session 7, please read through the AS 1100.101—1992 Technical drawing Part 101: General Principles which outlines the fundamental principles of technical drawing, covering aspects such as abbreviations, drawing materials and layouts, line types, lettering, dimensioning, and projection methods. This standard serves as a comprehensive guide for professionals in engineering, architecture, and related fields to ensure uniformity and clarity in technical drawings.
