In class activities

Session 7

 

Activity 1: Section 3 - Lines

Read through Section 3. Identify the different line weights and line styles listed by the Australian Standards on Technical Drawings

 

Activity 2: Section 4 - Letters, Numerals And Symbols

Read through Section 4. Identify the requirements around letters, numbers and symbols presented in architectural drawings.

Activity 3: Section 5 - Scales

Read through Section 5. Identify the different scales used for site plan, floor plan, section detail drawings.

 

Activity 4: Section 7 - Sections

Read through Section 7. Discuss the use of cutting plane and hatching.

 

Activity 5: Section 8 - Dimensioning

Read through the first 4 pages of Section 8. Identify elements of dimensioning, such as the number, dimension bar and offset distance between object and dimension bar.

Activity 6: Design Development

You will be given final project examples by your instructor. As a group, continue the studio mode design process. Your instructor will use the time to talk to each group about their design and provide feedback on the progress. Start to refine the design/final modelling at the stage. Construction drawings is not a must for the final project, but you can choose to include one or two.
