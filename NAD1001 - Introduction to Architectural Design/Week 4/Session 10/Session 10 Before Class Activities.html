Before class activities

Session 10

 

Watch: Why Great Architecture Should Tell A Story | Ole Scheeren

This video looks at architect Ole Scheeren's TED Talk, "Why great architecture should tell a story," where he emphasises the importance of integrating human experiences into architectural design. Scheeren discusses how buildings can narrate stories by reflecting the lives and interactions of the people who inhabit them.

 

Watch: Floating Cities, The LEGO House And Other Architectural Forms Of The Future | Bjarke Ingels

This video looks at architect Bjarke Ingels' TED Talk, "Floating cities, the LEGO House and other architectural forms of the future," where he explores innovative architectural projects that blend sustainability with human-centric design. Ingels discusses ventures like a waste-to-energy power plant doubling as a ski slope, the LEGO House in Denmark, and concepts for floating, sustainable cities adapted to climate change.

Watch: Steven Holl Architects: Ex Of In House

This video looks at Steven Holl Architects' "Ex of In House," a project that explores spatial energy and the ecology of place. The design features intersecting spherical and trapezoidal spaces, challenging conventional architectural language and commercial practices.
