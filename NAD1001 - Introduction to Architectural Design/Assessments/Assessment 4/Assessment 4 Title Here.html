What are the Assessment Details?

The Group Presentation (Assessment 4) provides an opportunity to showcase and communicate the design project you developed in Assessment 3. This task builds upon your completed project, allowing you to present key architectural design principles and insights gained throughout the group design process. The focus is on professionally articulating your design concepts while incorporating creative ideas, OHS standards, and cultural considerations to deliver a comprehensive and refined presentation.

Presenting as a group enriches the depth and impact of your project, fostering collaboration and shared ownership of outcomes. This assessment highlights your ability to communicate architectural design effectively and reinforces the foundational skills developed during your design journey. One group A1 poster is required for this assessment.

Please be reminded that submissions of the presentation file alone, without actual participation in the final presentation, will result in a score of 0 for criteria 3 and 4 in the rubric. Students will only be marked for criteria 1 and 2 based on the visual aspects of the presentation files. 

 

Assessment Instructions

To get started on your assessment task, please follow the below instructions.

  1. Refer to Assessment 3 (final project) for the content and visuals to support your presentation 
  2. Curate drawings generated in A3 format into a single A1 poster board 
  3. Prepare a 10–15-minute group presentation, using the final project drawings to aid your delivery

Assessment Criteria

The following levels of criteria will be used to grade this assessment task:

  • Criterion 1: Use of Visual Aids and Poster Design (25%) 
  • Criterion 2: Design Communication and Presentation Skills (25%) 
  • Criterion 3: Engagement and Presentation Delivery (25%) 
  • Criterion 4: Team Collaboration and Participation (25%)
