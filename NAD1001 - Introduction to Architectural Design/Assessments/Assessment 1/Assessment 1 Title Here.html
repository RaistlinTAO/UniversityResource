What are the Assessment Details?

The Creative Works (Assessment 1) requires you to synthesise key design concepts through three in-class creative design activities. In session 1, you will explore foundational design principles; session 2 focuses on culturally sustainable strategies; and session 3 involves site analysis to contextualise design within a specific environment. These activities form the foundation for a cohesive PDF portfolio, with a template provided to guide your work.

To complete this task, consolidate your work from each session by integrating visuals, critical reflections, and relevant notes. Refer to the materials from each session to support your portfolio development. These resources will assist you in refining your work into a polished, professional submission that demonstrates a clear understanding of these foundational design concepts. While AI can assist with brainstorming design ideas and finding precedents based on the design brief, all sketches for this assessment must be original and hand-drawn. 

 

Assessment Instructions

To get started on your assessment task, please follow the below instructions.

Refer to the materials in VUC for session 1 to session 3 

  1. Session 1 activity: Create three brainstorming sketches (floor plan, elevation, and section) based on the design brief 
  2. Session 2 activity: Complete a two-page precedent case study analysis, including annotated plans, photos of the precedent, relevant hand-sketched diagrams and notes 
  3. Session 3 activity: Produce two site sketches (site analysis and site plan) following the design brief  
  4. Scan all the hand-sketches 
  5. Download the provided template for this assessment and compile the three activities into the portfolio 
  6. Organise the portfolio and submit the completed assessment 
       

    Assessment Criteria

    The following levels of criteria will be used to grade this assessment task:

    • Criterion 1: Design Sketches and Key Design Principles (30%)
    • Criterion 2: Precedent Case Study and Critical Reflection (30%)  
    • Criterion 3: Contextual Site Analysis and Plan (30%) 
    • Criterion 4: Portfolio Visual and Presentation (10%)
