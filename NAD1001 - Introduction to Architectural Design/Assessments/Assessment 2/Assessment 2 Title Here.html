What are the Assessment Details?

The Individual Portfolio (Assessment 2) is designed to develop and showcase your skills in both 2D digital drawing and 3D modelling. Through this assessment, you will create a portfolio consisting of two parts: Part A, where you will complete three 2D digital drawing tasks with a focus on accuracy and technique to build foundational skills, and Part B, where you will complete three 3D modelling tasks to demonstrate your understanding of form, structure, design, and spatial relationships. 

To complete this assessment, refer to the checklist provided, which outlines the requirements for each task. Resources such as tutorials, software guides, and example portfolios are available to support your learning. These tools will help you refine your skills and produce a cohesive, professional portfolio that highlights your engagement with digital design practices. All tasks must be produced by you, and original digital files are required for submission. While you may use AI to assist with generating design ideas, the drawing and modelling practices must be completed independently. 

Assessment Instructions

To get started on your assessment task, please follow the below instructions.

  1. Refer to software training tutorials in VUC, you can find them in relevant sessions 
  2. Please complete the following three digital 2D drawing tasks (Part A):  
    1. Task 1: Basic 12 shapes 
    2. Task 2: Advanced 3 shapes  
    3. Task 3: Floor plan with dimensions and annotations 
    4. Screenshot of the Linkedin Learning certificate for AutoCAD - 2 hours.
  3. Curate all three tasks into a single digital file (e.g., DWG format) 
  4. Please complete the following three digital 3D modelling tasks (Part B): 
    1. Task sheet 1 
    2. Task sheet 2 
    3. Task sheet 3 
    4. Screenshor of the Different Design Platform certificate for Revit - 2 hours.
  5. Curate all three tasks to a single digital file (e.g., RVT format) 
  6. Submit the original digital drawing file (dwg.) and 3D modelling file (rvt.) 
  7. Meanwhile, finish two x 2 hours tutorials and obtain certificates 

   

Assessment Criteria

The following levels of criteria will be used to grade this assessment task:

  • Criterion 1: Task 1 (25%) 
  • Criterion 2: Task 2 (25%) 
  • Criterion 3: Task 3 (25%) 
  • Criterion 4: Design Thinking (25%) 
  • Essential - obtain certificates from the tutorial for each part of the assessment （Linked Learning 2 hours tutorial for AutoCAD and Different Design Platform 2 hours tutorial for Revit ）.
