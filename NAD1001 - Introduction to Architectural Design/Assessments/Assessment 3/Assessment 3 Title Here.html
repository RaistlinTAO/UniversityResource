What are the Assessment Details?

The Final Project (Assessment 3) offers you the chance to synthesise and apply your design skills by creating a building project using 3D modelling software. This assessment builds on your creative works and individual portfolio assessments, where you explored precedents, conducted site studies, and deepened your understanding of architectural design principles. Through this project, you will further develop your design and digital drawing skills, guided by provided scaffolds, quality assurance strategies, and documentation requirements. Your final project should reflect creative concepts while also meeting OHS standards and incorporating sustainable and culturally sensitive considerations. 

For guidance, consult the design brief, which provides detailed expectations for the project. Collaboration in pairs or groups of three is encouraged to enhance the design process, drawing on diverse perspectives to produce a more robust outcome. Consider adding advanced features, such as walkthroughs or site integration, to deepen the impact of your project. 

Apart from the Revit works, students must also include a series of original design sketches that demonstrate the progression of their ideas from initial concepts to final design. These sketches should reflect the development and refinement of the project over time, showcasing the student’s authentic design thinking and creative process. 

 

Assessment Instructions

To get started on your assessment task, please follow the below instructions.

  1. Refer to the design brief 
  2. Review precedents and site work completed in prior assessments 
  3. Gather design ideas in each session and progressively develop the project, using hand sketches or digital methods 
  4. Set deadlines to finalise all creative and design elements 
  5. Focus on digital modelling and visualisation to bring your project to completion 
  6. Produce the design drawing set and finalise your submission 

Submission Items 

  1. Item A: Original 3D Modelling File – e.g., RVT format 
  2. Item B: Design and Documentation Drawing Set PDF A3 – with title block, including: 
    1. Design sketches and diagrams associated with the design process and zoning 
    2. Floor plans (all levels) 
    3. Elevations (four views) 
    4. Sections (two views) 
    5. Details (optional) 
    6. Renders (two interior and two exterior)
