In class activities

Session 4

 

Activity 1: Create A Design Concept

Read through the webpage: 21 Architectural Concept Ideas and Examples

As an individual, create a design concept for the final project, then talk to your groupmates and see if you can develop a group design concept for the final- refer to the design brief again if you need. Share your design concept on the Miro Board

 

Activity 2: Initial Massing Study

As an individual choose between sketch/digital/model massing and conduct an initial massing study for the final project site/brief/concept in class. This way you and your groupmate will have some iterations of massing for the project. You can then work in group to select one massing. You are encouraged to think about the site analysis, initial design sketch and concept when conducing the massing study.  Be creative!!
