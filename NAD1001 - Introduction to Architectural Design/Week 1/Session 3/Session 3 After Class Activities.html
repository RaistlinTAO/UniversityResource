After Class Reading (Articles)

In addition to the textbook above, we also ask you to read through the familiarise yourself with the following: 

  • Planning and Environment Act 1987 - Legislation that governs land use planning in Victoria, setting the framework for planning schemes, permits, public participation, and enforcement.
  • Checklist 1 - Construction and/or Extension of a Single Dwelling or Multi Dwellings on a Lot - Outlines required documents for planning applications involving single or multi-dwelling construction or extensions, including forms, site plans, design responses, and fees.
  • Planning on a Page: Planning Victoria's liveable communities together - Outlines Victoria’s planning system, detailing policy development, land use controls, and decision-making processes. 
  • Understanding the Residential Development Standards (ResCode) - Explains Victoria’s residential development standards (ResCode), covering setbacks, building height, open space, and more.
  • Site Analysis - The "Site Analysis" electronic book prepared by Tifa contains thirty-six types of site analysis and studies, architectural examples, how-tos and tips.
