In class activities

Session 1

 

Activity 1: Key Design Stages And Core Services​

Read the following 2 readings (10 minutes):

  1. Core architectural services
  2. Client Architect Agreement (CAA2024)

After completing the readings, in groups, discuss and answer the following questions:

  • What are the key stages of architectural design?​

  • What stages are reflected in our weekly unit plan?​

  • What should designers complete in pre-design, schematic design, design development and documentation stages?

Share your groups answers on the Miro Board below.

 

Activity 2: Showcase Of Sample Final Outcomes

During Class, your instructor will go through some assessment examples so that you can start to visualise what you will be able to achieve by the end of this unit. We strongly recommend to read through all of the Assessment Information as soon as possible so you understand what is required throughout this unit.  

Activity 3: Identify A Building In Australia That Unsuccessfully Integrated OHS

As a group, identify one unsuccessful OHS integrated projects in Australia. Discuss what aspects of OHS were/were not integrated and develop a short group reflection on designer’s role in OHS. Consider the following questions:

  • How these learnt lessons can be applied in future architectural projects?
  • How these learnt lessons can be applied in own design?

Post your thoughts to the Miro Board.
