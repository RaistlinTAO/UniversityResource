In class activities

Session 2

 

Activity 1: Plagiarism In Architecture (Group)

Watch the following video that looks at how architects draw inspiration from existing designs, often leading to debates about originality and plagiarism. It examines notable examples where architectural works have closely resembled prior structures, highlighting the fine line between creative adaptation and copying. Once you have finished watching, discuss in groups the following:

  • Is precedent case study a form of copying?
  • How to avoid plagiarism in architecture when learning from precedents?

Post your groups thoughts to the Miro board.

 

Activity 2: Case Study Reading (Group)

From the precedent case study example in VUC (previous reading activity),&nbsp;Identify one or two key aspects of analysis – we will do this in groups for the next activity. 

 

Activity 3: Mini Precedent Case Study (Group)

Refer to the design brief of the final project: You are required to choose a building and conduct a mini precedent study in group​s. From the previous case study, each member in the group will focus on one identified aspect to analyse.​ Focus on the following:

  • Objectives: To identify and present key design principles and innovative features​.
  • Expected outcome: A short presentation and a visual display of your findings

Activity 4: Case Study Analysis (Individual)

Choose one case study from the readings and conduct a mini case study analysis. Focus on how the case study reflect cultural sensitivity in the design/ identify one culturally sustainable design strategies from the case study. Share your case study using Miro board.
